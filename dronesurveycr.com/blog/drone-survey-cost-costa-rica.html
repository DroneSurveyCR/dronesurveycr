This guide gives you real 2026 pricing, explains what drives the costs, and helps you understand when a drone survey will save you money compared to traditional methods.

What Affects Your Final Cost?

1. Travel & Mobilization

Costa Rica has diverse geography. If your property is in a remote area or requires multiple access points, we factor in travel time.

  • San José/Central Valley: No travel fee (we're based here)
  • Surrounding provinces (Cartago, Alajuela, Heredia): $0–150 travel fee
  • Pacific coast (Puntarenas, Guanacaste): $200–400 travel fee
  • Caribbean (Limón): $300–500 travel fee
  • Remote mountain locations: Custom quote (may include overnight stays)

2. Terrain & Site Conditions

Dense jungle, mountain terrain, and coastal cliffs require more careful flight planning and sometimes multiple flights.

  • Flat agricultural land: Standard pricing
  • Hilly/forested terrain: +15–25% (extra flights for vegetation penetration with lidar)
  • Steep slopes (>30°): +30–50% (multiple passes, more processing time)
  • Mixed terrain with water: Standard to +20% depending on complexity

3. Data Requirements

Different projects need different outputs. Our base pricing includes standard deliverables, but specialized needs cost more:

  • Standard package: Orthomosaic, DEM, point cloud, basic shapefile — included
  • 3D model/mesh: +$200–400 (depending on size)
  • High-resolution 1cm/pixel ortho: +$150–300
  • Classified point cloud (terrain vs. vegetation): +$300–600
  • Volume calculations (earthwork estimates): +$250–500
  • CAD-ready deliverables (DXF/DWG): +$200–350

4. Weather & Timing

During Costa Rica's rainy season (May–November), cloud cover and rain can delay flights. Dry season (December–April) typically requires no weather premiums.

  • Dry season: Standard pricing
  • Rainy season (wet forecasts/rescheduling): May add $100–300 for multiple flight attempts
  • Same-day or rush processing: +$200–400

5. Equipment Choice

We use DJI Matrice 300 RTK with Zenmuse L1 for lidar and Zenmuse H30T for photogrammetry. Both are enterprise-grade, but lidar costs more to operate and process.

  • Photogrammetry (RGB imagery): Lower cost, great for orthomosaics and 3D models
  • Lidar (laser scanning): 15–40% higher cost, but penetrates vegetation and provides terrain under trees

Hidden Costs to Consider

Some costs only appear during project planning:

DGAC Permits

Commercial drone operations in Costa Rica require permits from the Dirección General de Aviación Civil (DGAC). We handle all permitting for you—included in our base pricing. This alone saves you $300–600 if you were hiring someone else.

Data Processing & Storage

Raw lidar and photogrammetry data is massive (2–10 GB per flight). Our pricing includes processing, cloud storage, and web portal access for 12 months. Extended storage is $50/month per project.

Rush or After-Hours Services

If you need results within 24 hours, expect a 30–50% rush fee. Weekend and evening flights cost 25% more.

Software Licenses for Analysis

While we deliver standard formats (GeoTIFF, point clouds, shapefiles), some advanced analysis tools (ArcGIS, specialized 3D software) require separate licenses. We can recommend tools for different budgets.
