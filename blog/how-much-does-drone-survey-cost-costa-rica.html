What Factors Affect Drone Survey Costs in Costa Rica?

1. Property Size

The larger the area, the more time needed for both the flight mission and data processing. Prices are generally scaled per hectare, though small properties have a minimum base cost to cover mobilization and fixed overhead. For a complete overview of what an aerial survey in Costa Rica involves, see our detailed guide.

2. Lidar vs Photogrammetry

Lidar is more expensive than photogrammetry because the equipment is more specialized — but it's often the only way to get accurate terrain data through Costa Rica's dense forests. If your land has heavy tree cover or jungle, lidar is usually required. For open agricultural land or construction sites, photogrammetry offers excellent results at a lower cost. Read our full Lidar vs Photogrammetry comparison guide to understand which is right for your project.

3. Terrain Complexity

Mountainous terrain, steep hillsides, or irregular boundaries take longer to plan and fly than flat open land. Complex topography also requires more ground control points, which adds processing time and cost.

4. Location in Costa Rica

Travel time to your property affects cost. Projects in the Greater San José area or within a couple hours of our base are included in standard pricing. Remote areas — like the Osa Peninsula, the Caribbean coast, or very isolated mountain zones — may incur a travel or accommodation fee, which we'll clearly itemize in your quote.

5. Deliverable Requirements

A basic orthomosaic and topographic map are standard. If you need engineering-grade point clouds, BIM models, volumetric calculations, or cadastral boundary files for CATASTRO submission, additional processing time applies. Our LiDAR mapping service and photogrammetry service pages detail exactly what deliverables are included with each survey type.

6. Urgency

Standard delivery is 3–7 business days after the flight. Rush processing (48-hour turnaround) is available at a premium for time-sensitive projects like active real estate closings.

Is a Drone Survey Worth the Cost in Costa Rica?

In almost every case, yes — and here's why:

A traditional topographic survey by a licensed engineer in Costa Rica typically costs significantly more, takes longer, and provides less detail. A drone survey covers the same ground in a fraction of the time, with better spatial coverage and digital data you can use in any GIS or CAD application.

For real estate transactions, a drone survey can reveal terrain issues, drainage problems, or boundary discrepancies that could void a deal or cost far more to fix after purchase. For construction, accurate pre-grading data prevents costly earthwork surprises. The survey typically pays for itself many times over.

How is the price calculated for larger properties?

It's simple: $1,000 USD for the first 5 hectares, then $80 USD per additional hectare. For example, a 20-hectare property would be $1,000 + (15 × $80) = $2,200 USD. Properties over 100 hectares qualify for special pricing — contact us for a custom quote.
