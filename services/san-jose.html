Drone Survey Services in San José

LiDAR mapping, photogrammetry, and topographic surveys for Costa Rica's Central Valley — the country's commercial and development hub

Why San José Province Needs Professional Drone Surveys

San José province sits at the heart of Costa Rica's Central Valley, an elevated plateau between 1,000 and 1,400 meters above sea level surrounded by volcanic ridges. Home to over 1.4 million people, the province contains the country's densest concentration of construction activity, real estate development, and commercial infrastructure. From the high-rise corridors of Escazú and Santa Ana to the expanding residential zones of Desamparados and Curridabat, urban growth here is constant and complex.

The terrain across San José is deceptively challenging for traditional surveys. Deep river canyons cut through the metropolitan area — the Río Torres, Río María Aguilar, and Río Tiribí create steep ravines that are difficult to access on foot. Hillside developments in areas like Escazú, Pérez Zeledón, and the slopes of Cerro de la Muerte demand accurate slope analysis before any construction begins.

Drone surveys are essential for keeping pace with San José's rapid urbanization. Whether you are developing a condominium complex in Rohrmoser, mapping a coffee farm in Tarrazú, or planning road infrastructure along the Circunvalación, our LiDAR and photogrammetry services deliver centimeter-accurate data faster and more affordably than ground crews working through congested streets and steep terrain.

We operate throughout all 20 cantons of San José province, from the urban core through the agricultural zones of Los Santos and the mountain passes near Cerro Chirirpó. Our DGAC-licensed pilots and DJI Matrice 300 RTK system are equipped to handle restricted airspace near Juan Santamaría Airport approaches and the complex flight planning that urban environments require.

Frequently Asked Questions — Drone Surveys in San José

Can you fly drones in San José's urban areas?

Yes. Our pilots hold DGAC licenses and we coordinate with aviation authorities when operating near restricted airspace, including the Juan Santamaría Airport approach corridors. We handle all flight authorizations required for urban drone operations in the Greater Metropolitan Area.

How much does a drone survey cost in San José?

LiDAR surveys start at $1,000 base fee plus $80 per hectare. Urban sites in the Central Valley typically cost less for mobilization since our team is based in the region. A typical 5-hectare construction site survey runs approximately $1,400. Contact us for a precise quote.

What is the turnaround time for processed data?

Standard delivery is 48-72 hours after data collection. For construction monitoring projects requiring regular surveys, we offer scheduled programs with same-week delivery. Rush processing is available for time-sensitive real estate transactions and permitting deadlines.

Can LiDAR survey hillside properties with dense vegetation?

Absolutely. This is one of LiDAR's greatest advantages for San José province. Properties on the slopes of Escazú, the hills above Curridabat, or the coffee-growing highlands are often covered in vegetation that hides the true terrain. LiDAR penetrates this canopy to reveal accurate ground elevations for engineering design.

Do you cover all of San José province, including rural areas?

Yes. We serve all 20 cantons of San José province, from the dense urban core through the agricultural zones of Tarrazú, Dota, and Puriscal, to the mountainous southern reaches near Pérez Zeledón and San Gerardo de Dota.
