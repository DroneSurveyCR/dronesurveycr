What We Offer

Drone Survey Costa Rica specializes in high-precision aerial surveying and mapping using the latest drone technology. Whether you need topographic mapping, 3D models, GPS positioning, or professional aerial media, we have the expertise and equipment to deliver survey-quality results.

LiDAR Drone Mapping

Precision topographic surveys with ±1-3cm accuracy. Our LiDAR technology penetrates forest canopy to map ground terrain beneath dense vegetation.

Perfect for: Land development, engineering projects, forestry, infrastructure planning.

3D Photogrammetry

GPS Geopositioning
